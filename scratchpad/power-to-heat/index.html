Power to Heat

Heating with locally produced hydrogen.

Key Features

This is what makes power to heat unique.

Solar heat in winter

Throughout the year, the software continuously monitors grid prices, the availability of green energy, weather forecasts, and your specific circumstances. It uses this information to optimize the system's performance for your needs making sure you can store the surplus solar energy generated in summer and use it for heating in winter.

Replaces gas

Hydrogen is a gas with characteristics very similar to natural gas. With Power-to-Heat, we burn the hydrogen directly replacing your need for conventional gas.

Mixing with gas

Hydrogen is so similar to natural gas that it can be mixed with it, allowing you to start using hydrogen without needing to switch over completely right away.

Any temperature any usecase

Burning hydrogen can generate very high temperatures, making it suitable for situations where heat pumps or electric heating are not viable.

There is a wide range of hydrogen boilers, gas mixers, and hydrogen-compatible heat pumps available for both industrial and domestic use. We work with a variety of suppliers to match your specific needs.

Hardly any energy loss

When converting between electricity and hydrogen, heat is released. This heat is often considered "energy loss." In our system, however, the released heat is recovered and stored resulting in less than 15% energy loss and making the entire process highly efficient.

A year-round solution

In summer, there is often an excess of solar power, while energy consumption is relatively low. In winter, days are shorter and temperatures lower most energy use then goes toward heating. With power to heat, we convert electricity from solar panels into hydrogen, which can be stored and used for heating in winter. Click through the pictures to see how the system behaves across different seasons.

During summer, solar panels generate more energy than you consume. At these moments, we convert the excess electricity into hydrogen and store it in tanks for later use. The system includes a battery that balances your electricity usage throughout the day, ensuring only the surplus energy is converted into hydrogen while also extending the electrolyzer’s operating hours. While generating hydrogen, some heat is released and recovered. This heat can be used for hot water typically enough to provide showers for a family.

In winter we use the hydrogen to heat your home. We use hydrogen boilers making sure you are warm even if temperartures get very low. Electricity prices still vary a lot in the winter, and can still be negative. At those moments we still make hydrogen to keep your tanks filled.
