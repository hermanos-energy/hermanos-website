Power to Drive

Drive a boat or vehicle on your own hydrogen.

Key Features

This is what makes power to drive unique.

Never out of fuel

The hydrogen used to fuel your vehicle is produced on-site, so you don’t depend on external fuel suppliers, and your vehicle doesn’t need to leave the premises to refuel. Our software monitors grid prices, the availability of renewable energy, and the amount of fuel left in your tanks. It not only ensures you always have fuel but also chooses the most cost-effective moments to produce it.

More than just fuel

During hydrogen production, we recover waste heat and use a battery to extend the electrolyzer’s runtime.

Drive anywhere

Diesel-powered vehicles are not welcome in zero-emission zones like nature reserves. Battery-powered vehicles are often not feasible either, as their weight can cause them to sink in muddy terrain. In these cases, hydrogen-powered vehicles are the only practical option and they also offer a much greater range.

Easy refueling

Our refueling station is easy to use, simple, and reliable. The experience is very similar to refueling with petrol or diesel.

We use the high pressure in the storage tanks to fill your vehicle, slowly releasing the gas to ensure safety and reliability. We can fuel vehicles up to 300 bar, and it takes around fifteen minutes to fill your vehicle.

We manage the entire process, from producing the hydrogen to compressing and refueling, so we can ensure the tanks are always at the right pressure to fill your vehicle efficiently

50% cheapter than diesel

Your own solar energy is by far the cheapest energy available. We use it to produce hydrogen for around €3 per kilogram. A typical car can drive approximately 100 km on just one kilogram of hydrogen. This makes hydrogen a much more affordable option then diesel as long as it's produced locally by you.

Buying hydrogen at a commercial fuel station cost around €21 per kilogram.  Making your own is 7 times cheapter.

Fuel is made while you use the vehicle

During a sunny summer day, energy is cheap and green but that’s often the exact moment you want to use your vehicle. We produce hydrogen when there’s an abundance of green energy and store it in tanks, so you can refuel later. Whether you have a vehicle you use daily or only during certain seasons, we always choose the right moments throughout the year to produce hydrogen ensuring it’s available when you need it. Click the pictures below to see how the system works at different times

While you're out working during the day and using your vehicle, the electrolyzer produces hydrogen using the energy generated by your solar panels. By the time you return from a hard day’s work, your hydrogen tanks are full and ready to use.

When the workday is over, the sun is down, and energy prices are high your vehicle is fueled by the green and cheap hydrogen produced during the day. Ready to go again tomorrow.
